# 👥 User Personas & Strategic Use Cases

This document defines the 10 core personas we serve and the tactical workflows that provide them with "Elite Intelligence."

---

## 🌐 The Hub Researcher (Web / Pre-Festival)

### 1. The Artist Superfan
- **Goal**: Track a specific artist across the entire summer circuit.
- **Workflow**: Uses **Global Search** on the Hub root.
- **Tactical Advantage**: Cross-festival data aggregation on a single domain.

### 2. The Vibe-First Vacationer
- **Goal**: Find the "right" festival based on non-musical preferences.
- **Workflow**: Uses the **Global Vibe Scout** (AI Matchmaker).
- **Tactical Advantage**: High-reasoning AI that understands "Tribes" (e.g., Global Nomad vs. Main Stage Hero).

### 3. The Budget Conscious Scout
- **Goal**: Compare cost of attendance (Food/Shuttle) between festivals.
- **Workflow**: Deep-links into the **Food Finder** of multiple festivals via the Hub.
- **Tactical Advantage**: Unified data schemas allow for direct utility comparison.

## 📱 The Tactical Attendee (Android / On-Site)

### 4. The "Deep Crowd" Techno Head
- **Goal**: Find similar music in a 0-signal, high-density environment.
- **Workflow**: Uses **Offline AI Scout** (Gemma 4) to find the next high-BPM set.
- **Tactical Advantage**: 100% Local Inference; zero latency, zero battery drain from searching for signal.

### 5. The "Walking Past a Stage" Explorer
- **Goal**: Identify an unknown artist by their sound.
- **Workflow**: Taps the **Acoustic Vibe Scout**.
- **Tactical Advantage**: Native Multimodal AI (Audio-to-Identity) on-device.

### 6. The Overwhelmed First-Timer
- **Goal**: Simplify a 200+ artist lineup into something manageable.
- **Workflow**: Taps **Radar Focus** (e.g., "DELTA DISTRICT").
- **Tactical Advantage**: Config-driven "Territory" filtering.

### 7. The "Lost in the Dust" Navigator
- **Goal**: Find essentials (Water/SOS) when visibility is low or GPS is failing.
- **Workflow**: Uses the **Tactical Map** (SVG).
- **Tactical Advantage**: High-fidelity, data-independent vector maps.
### 8. The International Nomad
- **Goal**: Communicate basic needs at a local festival.
- **Workflow**: Uses the **Phrases** tab in the Toolkit.
- **Tactical Advantage**: Context-aware dictionary terms injected via JSON.

### 9. The "Last Minute Change" Realist
- **Goal**: Get immediate schedule updates when an artist cancels.
- **Workflow**: Taps **REFRESH DATA** in the Toolkit.
- **Tactical Advantage**: Live Refresh Sync via Room DB.
