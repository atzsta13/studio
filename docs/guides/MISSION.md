# 🎯 The Mission: Sziget Insider 2026

## 🌟 The Vision
Sziget Insider exists to empower every "Szitizen" with an intelligent, offline-first survival and discovery engine. We believe that technology should enhance—not distract from—the raw energy of the Island of Freedom.

## 🚀 The Mission Statement
To provide the most robust, private, and intelligent festival companion app ever built. We aim to solve the three core anxieties of a festival-goer:
1. **Discovery Anxiety**: "What should I see that I don't know yet?" (Solved by AI Scout & Spotify Match).
2. **Survival Anxiety**: "How do I find my tent/water/friends with no signal?" (Solved by Offline Maps, SOS, & GPS Tent Finder).
3. **Logistics Anxiety**: "How much am I spending? When is the next set?" (Solved by HUF Converter & Clash-detecting Timetable).

## 🛡️ Core Values
- **Offline First**: If it doesn't work without a 5G signal, it's a failure.
- **Privacy First**: User data (Favorites, Memories, GPS) stays strictly in `localStorage`.
- **Performance First**: Navigating between 80+ artists must be sub-second.
- **Vibe Focused**: We prioritize the "energy" of the island over static data.

## 📈 North Star Metrics
- **Zero Signal Utility**: 100% functionality of maps and guides during network congestion.
- **Discovery Rate**: Users finding at least 3 new artists via the AI Scout.
- **Stress Reduction**: Users successfully navigating back to camp using the Tent Finder.
